# TechGuru Network & Data Solutions

## Register

**Brand** — This is a B2B IT services company website. Design IS the product: the website must project technical authority, trustworthiness, and premium quality to enterprise clients.

## Product Purpose

TechGuru is a Hong Kong-based IT infrastructure company specializing in network, security, and virtualization solutions. The website serves as the primary digital presence for:
- Showcasing services (Build / Run / Protect)
- Lead generation through contact forms and ticket submission
- Establishing credibility via case studies and partner logos
- Providing support portal for existing clients

## Target Users

- **Primary**: IT managers and CTOs at SMBs/enterprises in Hong Kong and Southeast Asia looking for infrastructure modernization
- **Secondary**: Existing clients needing support (ticket system)
- **Context**: Evaluating vendors, comparing solutions, seeking reliable long-term IT partners

## Desired Emotional Response

- **Confidence**: "These people know what they're doing"
- **Trust**: "This is a professional, established company"
- **Clarity**: "I understand what they offer and how to engage"

## Brand Personality (3 words)

Professional · Technical · Reliable

## Anti-References (what NOT to look like)

- Generic SaaS landing page with gradient CTAs and fake metrics
- Cluttered IT reseller site with product grids and price lists
- Overly playful or casual design (this is enterprise IT, not a startup)
- Template-feeling identical card grids everywhere

## Key Services

1. **Build** — Network infrastructure design and deployment (H3C, Huawei, Sangfor, Ruijie)
2. **Run** — Ongoing management and monitoring (routing, switching, wireless, SD-WAN)
3. **Protect** — Cybersecurity solutions (Fortinet, Sophos, firewalls, zero-trust)

## Competitive Position

- Local HK expertise with enterprise-grade vendor partnerships
- VMware alternative specialist (KVM, Proxmox, Nutanix)
- End-to-end: design → deploy → manage → protect

## Accessibility Needs

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Keyboard navigation support
- Screen reader compatibility
- Reduced motion support (already implemented)
