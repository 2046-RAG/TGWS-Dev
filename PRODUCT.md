# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary**: Enterprise IT decision-makers and technical teams in Southeast Asia (primarily Philippines, HK, TW, overseas Chinese markets)

**Context**: Evaluating IT infrastructure vendors, seeking cybersecurity solutions, exploring AI adoption, or migrating from VMware

**Job to be done**: 
- Evaluate TechGuru as a trusted IT solutions integrator
- Understand product offerings (Build/Run/Protect framework)
- Request support or submit tickets
- Contact for consultation

## Product Purpose

TechGuru Network & Data Solutions Inc. (泰谷网数科技) is an Asian IT solutions integrator covering:
- Cybersecurity (NGFW, WAF, EDR, NDR, MDR)
- Network infrastructure (routing, switching, wireless)
- Cloud computing & virtualization (VMware alternative, HCI, cloud migration)
- AI/AIGC solutions (AIGC, AI Coding, AI Agent, AI Adoption)
- Managed services & business continuity

**Success looks like**: Enterprise clients trust TechGuru for their Build. Run. Protect. IT infrastructure needs

## Brand Personality

**Voice**: Professional, trustworthy, cutting-edge, AI-driven

**3-word personality**: Confident, Expert, Forward-thinking

**Emotional goals**: 
- Confidence in technical capability
- Trust in partnership reliability
- Excitement about AI-powered future

## Anti-references

**What this should NOT look like**:
- Generic SaaS landing page (cream backgrounds, serif fonts, terracotta accents)
- Overly corporate/boring enterprise site
- Startup-y playful/toyish design
- Dark hacker/cyberpunk aesthetic (too aggressive)
- AI-generated default (gradient text, glassmorphism everywhere, numbered section markers)

## Design Principles

1. **Build. Run. Protect.** - Three-pillar framework drives all content organization
2. **Show, don't tell** - Real case studies, quantified results, partner logos
3. **Expert confidence** - Professional but not stiff; cutting-edge but accessible
4. **AI-first positioning** - Forward-thinking without being gimmicky
5. **Regional focus** - Southeast Asia/Philippines market with international standards

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance required
- Keyboard navigation support
- Color contrast ≥4.5:1 for body text
- Reduced motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Bilingual: English (primary) + Traditional Chinese (secondary)
- Responsive: Mobile-first, breakpoints at sm/md/lg/xl

## Key Messages

1. "Asia's leading IT solutions integrator"
2. "Build. Run. Protect." - Three-pillar framework
3. "Your Trusted IT Partner in Asia"
4. VMware migration specialist (5 alternatives)
5. AI adoption services (not AI company, but IT integrator with AI expertise)

## Visual Identity

**Color Strategy**: Restrained with committed accents
- Primary: #00D4FF (cyan/electric blue)
- Accent: #7B61FF (purple)
- Success: #22C55E (green)
- Background: #F4F4F5 (light gray)
- Surface: #FAFAFA (white)
- Foreground: #18181B (near-black)

**Typography**: 
- Heading: HelveticaNowDisplay-Medium
- Body: HelveticaNowDisplayW01-Rg
- Fallbacks: Inter, Arial, sans-serif

**Design System**:
- Cards: `.card` class with border, border-radius 16px, hover effects
- Buttons: `.btn-primary` (gradient), `.btn-secondary` (outlined)
- Sections: `.section-title`, `.section-subtitle`
- Animations: `.reveal`, `.anim-fade-up`, `.anim-card`, `.glow`
